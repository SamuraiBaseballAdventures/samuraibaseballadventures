Tour Experience

Six Unforgettable
Experiences

Every tour is handcrafted around six signature experiences — each offering a window into a side of Japan that most travelers never see.

01

NPB Live Games

Feel the electric atmosphere of Japan's professional baseball league. Synchronized cheer squads, brass bands, stadium food — an experience unlike anything in the US.

02

Koshien Stadium

Visit the most sacred ground in Japanese baseball. Whether watching the high school tournament or touring the iconic ivy-covered walls — Koshien moves every baseball soul.

03

Baseball Equipment Factory Tour

Step inside Japanese glove and bat factories where master craftsmen have been perfecting their trade for generations. Witness the art of baseball equipment making up close.

04

Glove Making Experience

Sit alongside a master artisan and create your own custom baseball glove by hand. A one-of-a-kind souvenir you'll treasure for life, crafted in the Japanese tradition.

05

Baseball Equipment Shop Tour

Your expert guide takes you to Japan's finest baseball equipment stores — many unknown to outsiders — where you'll find gloves, bats, and gear unavailable anywhere else in the world.

06

Play & Practice with Japanese Teams

For players: join joint practice sessions and exhibition games with local Japanese clubs. Learn their legendary techniques, discipline, and baseball philosophy on the field.

Sample Schedule

9 Days in Japan —
Day by Day

Every tour is customized to your group. Below is a representative 9-day itinerary to give you a feel for the journey.

Day 01

Arrival in Tokyo

Land at Narita or Haneda International Airport. Private transfer to your hotel in central Tokyo. Evening welcome dinner with your guide — meet the team and get oriented for the adventure ahead.

✈ Arrival🍜 Welcome Dinner🏨 Tokyo Hotel
Day 02

NPB Game Day — Tokyo

Morning sightseeing around Tokyo (Shibuya, Asakusa, or Akihabara). Evening: premium seats at a Tokyo NPB game — Giants, Swallows, or Marines. Experience the synchronized cheer culture, stadium food, and electric energy of Japanese professional baseball.

⚾ NPB Game🗼 Sightseeing🍣 Local Food
Day 03

Baseball Equipment Deep Dive

Your expert guide takes you through Tokyo's legendary baseball equipment district — shops not listed online, brands not exported, gear you can't find anywhere else. A heaven for players and fans alike. Afternoon free for personal shopping.

🛒 Equipment Shopping🎯 Exclusive Access
Day 04

Shinkansen West — Osaka

Board the iconic Shinkansen bullet train and head to Osaka. Check in and explore Dotonbori's vibrant street food scene. Evening NPB game at Kyocera Dome Osaka watching the Orix Buffaloes or visiting Hanshin Tigers.

🚄 Shinkansen⚾ NPB Game🦑 Dotonbori
Day 05

Koshien — The Sacred Ground

A pilgrimage to Hanshin Koshien Stadium — the most revered venue in Japanese baseball. Tour the hallowed grounds, learn its 100-year history, and if the calendar aligns, witness a high school game where young players pour their entire souls onto the field.

🏟 Koshien Stadium⚾ High School Baseball
Day 06

Glove Artisan Workshop

Visit a master craftsman's workshop where baseball gloves are born by hand. Watch decades of skill in action — every stitch deliberate, every cut precise. For players: create your own fully custom glove to take home. For fans: observe and enjoy a hands-on stitching demo.

🧤 Glove Workshop🔨 Hands-on Creation
Day 07

Joint Practice & Exhibition Game

Players step onto the diamond alongside a Japanese team for joint practice — drills, fielding, batting. Then: an exhibition game under the afternoon sun. Fans and families cheer from the stands. An evening celebration dinner brings both teams together.

⚾ Exhibition Game🤝 Cultural Exchange🎉 Celebration
Day 08

Kyoto — Ancient Japan

A full day in ancient Kyoto. Wander Fushimi Inari's thousand torii gates, stroll Arashiyama's bamboo grove, and taste sake at a traditional brewery. A day to breathe in the Japan that exists beyond the stadium.

⛩ Kyoto🎋 Arashiyama🍶 Cultural Day
Day 09

Farewell & Departure

A free morning for last-minute shopping, reflection, or a final bowl of ramen. Private airport transfer. You leave Japan as a different person — carrying the spirit of Japanese baseball home with you.

✈ Departure💛 Until Next Time

Our Philosophy
